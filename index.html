About

      
For the love of golf.

      
Albie is an open source caddie. It reads everything ever written, filmed or mapped about a course, argues with itself like a tour caddie, and hands you ranked ways to play the shot in front of you.

    

    
    

    
Every glyph is real course data. Run your finger through it.

    

    

      
How Albie thinks

      

        
01Ingest. Official hole guides, tour coverage, caddie videos, member forums, OpenStreetMap geometry.

        
02Triangulate. Nothing counts on one source. Conflicts get flagged, never merged.

        
03Compile. Everything becomes IF situation THEN advice, per hole, per tee, per pin.

        
04Personalize. Your carries, misses and weaknesses re-rank every option. Never a club you can't hit.

        
05Learn. Your call ratings and rounds become ground truth in the next update.

      

    

    

      
Honesty, by design

      
Every fact carries a source tag and a freshness date. Confidence is computed, not claimed: check any course's trust card. What Albie doesn't know is displayed, never hidden.
