01

    
What this is

    
Albie is an AI caddie built on ingested course knowledge. Before you ever open it, it has read the official hole guides, watched the tour coverage and the caddie videos, mined the member forums, and traced the course from survey maps. Then it takes your real carries and misses and argues out every shot the way a good caddie would: ranked options, one club, one target, one warning.

    
No static game plans. Everything is conditional on wind, pin, lie, and where your ball actually sits. Every fact carries its source and its freshness date. What Albie doesn't know, it says out loud.

How it thinks

    

      
01 · INGESTEverything ever written. Official hole guides, tour broadcasts, caddie interviews, member forums, OpenStreetMap geometry, elevation surveys.

      
02 · TRIANGULATENothing counts on one source. Facts need independent agreement. Conflicts get flagged and shown, never silently merged.

      
03 · COMPILEIF situation, THEN advice. Knowledge becomes conditional calls, per hole, per tee, per pin, per wind. Pre-argued, harness-tested.

      
04 · PERSONALIZEYour numbers decide. Your carries, misses, strengths and nerve re-rank every option. Never a club you can't hit.

      
05 · LEARNYour rounds become ground truth. Every rated call and exported round feeds the next version's brain. The caddie you test this month is not the caddie you get next month.

04

    
The vision

    
Shot-history apps know nothing about a course you've never played. That's exactly when you need a caddie most.

    
Albie is built for destination golf first. The trip to St Andrews, the one round at a course you've waited years for, where local knowledge is the difference between a memory and a card full of doubles. The same machine then goes where no publisher will ever go: the local courses nobody writes yardage books for, built up with the people who play them every week.

    

      
Open source, soon. The app first, then the ingestion pipeline as the data licensing sweep finishes. Build courses with us.

      
Honesty by design. Sourced facts, flagged conflicts, computed confidence, visible staleness. A caddie you can call out.

      
Free, for the love of golf. The app stays free. The plan is community, not a paywall.

    

  


  

  

    
05

    
Try it, break it, send it back

    

      
01Open the app on your phone and set up your bag. Honest guesses beat no data. Two minutes, nothing leaves your phone.

      
02Take it out for a real round, or a sim session. Tap the map where your ball is and watch it re-think the shot.

      
03Rate the calls, and tap the option you actually played. Disagreeing with Albie is the most useful thing you can do.

      
04Export your data from the Log tab and send it back. It becomes ground truth in the next caddie update.
